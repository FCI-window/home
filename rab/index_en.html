Have You Ever Read the Bible?

Have you ever read the Bible aloud in your own voice?

What If You Read the Bible in Your Own Voice?

⌄

Have you ever finished reading the Bible but felt as though you had not really read it?

Have you ever felt God's Word pass by too quickly?

What if those words stayed with you in your own voice?
What if you read them aloud as if you were reading them directly to someone?

Would that change the way you read?

03

How Will the Words You Read Stay With You?

⌄

Have you ever listened to the Bible read by an AI voice? It can feel a little distant, can't it?

The Bible read by a professional narrator or a well-known voice can be meaningful, too.

But what if you could leave your own voice for your children and grandchildren?

What if the words you read today could remain in your voice for a long time?

Let's Try Record Aloud Bible.

⌄

Read aloud in your own voice, one verse at a time.
