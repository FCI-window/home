03

Record Aloud Bible: A New Way to Read the Bible.

⌄

Point to each verse as you read. Seeing it, saying it, and hearing it helps you focus fully on God's Word.

Even three minutes a day can become time set apart for you and God. Try Record Aloud Bible.

The goal is not only to keep a recording, but to make time to focus on God's Word.

If someone hears you and you feel awkward, just say, “I'm recording.” They will understand.

Start with One Verse Today.

⌄

The words you read are recorded and saved in your own voice.

Back up and restore your recordings with Google Drive, even when you change phones.

At church or in a small group, read together, organize Bible reading challenges, and encourage one another.

One verse becomes a chapter, and one chapter becomes a book—God's Word speaking personally to you.

with your hands, eyes, voice, and ears.
