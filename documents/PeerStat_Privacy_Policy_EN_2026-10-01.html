PeerStat Privacy Policy

Version: privacy-2026-10-01 | Effective date: October 1, 2026

FCI Inc. (the “Company”) complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws of the Republic of Korea and values the privacy of users of PeerStat (the “Service”). This Policy explains the personal information processed by the Company in connection with the Service, the purposes and retention periods, and how users may exercise their rights.

Article 1 (Service characteristics)

  1. PeerStat is a work-oriented service in which members of a company or organization evaluate one another’s work, and the results are provided to an administrator or a result recipient designated by the administrator.
  2. Administrators use Firebase email-and-password accounts. Members participate without a separate member account by entering an evaluation-group code, name, and position.
  3. Members are not shown how other members evaluated them. In principle, administrators cannot view individual evaluation source data or the relationship between evaluators and evaluated persons.
  4. The Service is not available to persons under 14. The Company does not collect dates of birth and processes age based on the user’s confirmation.

Article 3 (Separation of evaluation source data and access restrictions)

  1. The Company is designed to process participant-identifying information separately from evaluation source data.
  2. Administrators and sub-accounts may check member participation status and overall completion, but cannot access individual evaluation source data or evaluator-identification relationships.
  3. A member may view or edit only the evaluation the member submitted and cannot access evaluations or results concerning the member submitted by others.
  4. A result recipient may view only aggregated results and reports for an authenticated evaluation group and cannot access the source evaluation data.

Article 5 (Leaving and deletion by a member)

  1. If a member leaves before an evaluation begins, the server deletes the member’s display name, participation-token hash, app-installation-identifier hash, push token, completion status, and age/Terms confirmation records.
  2. Participation-group information and temporary evaluation data stored on the device are also deleted.
  3. After an evaluation begins, a member cannot leave the group in order to preserve evaluation integrity.
  4. If the member rejoins the same group, a new participantId and participation token are issued.
  5. Deleting a group card on the device after completion deletes information on the device and is distinct from retroactively deleting server-side evaluation data that has become subject to retention.

Article 6 (Administrator account deletion and data retention)

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of a PeerStat administrator account, sign in to the app or administrator web service and use the “Delete account” option. If you have deleted the app or cannot sign in, request deletion by emailing fci.window@gmail.com. Include the account email address and the subject “PeerStat account deletion request”. Do not send your password or full payment-method information by email.

After deletion, the Firebase Authentication administrator account and administrator profile information that is unnecessary for account identification are deleted or de-identified. Data relating to free evaluation groups may be retained for 30 days after the relevant processing purpose ends, and data relating to paid evaluation groups may be retained for 3 years. Payment and transaction records may be retained for the period required by applicable laws or payment-provider policies. Data is deleted without delay after the retention period ends.

Questions about submitting or processing a deletion request may be sent to fci.window@gmail.com.

  1. An administrator may request account deletion. After re-authentication, the Company deletes the Firebase Authentication administrator account.
  2. Even after account deletion, evaluation groups, source data, payment information, and result-recipient information already created may be retained for the periods in this Policy.
  3. A former administrator cannot use the administrator account to access those groups or results again.
  4. Result-recipient credentials configured for a completed group may remain according to the retention period and settings, and the result recipient may view results separately from the administrator account.
  5. Administrator profile information, push tokens, and information unnecessary for account identification are deleted or de-identified.

Article 7 (Destruction)

  1. Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose processing purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay.
  2. Electronic files are securely deleted so they cannot be recovered.
  3. When a member leaves an evaluation group, server participation information and device participation and temporary data are deleted.
  4. Payment and transaction records required by law are destroyed after the statutory retention period ends.

Article 9 (Provision to third parties)

In principle, the Company does not provide personal information to third parties. However, where a user chooses to use an external service, payment verification is required, or a request is made under law by an investigative authority, the Company may provide or process necessary information to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Article 10 (Rights and how to exercise them)

  1. An administrator may request access to, correction, deletion of, or suspension of processing of the administrator’s personal information.
  2. A member may make a personal-information request by providing the evaluation-group code, display name used, and other information necessary to verify the member.
  3. The Company verifies the requester and scope of the request and processes it under applicable law.
  4. When a member leaves an evaluation group, the relevant participation information may be deleted through the Service.

Article 11 (Security measures)

The Company takes necessary technical and organizational measures, including access restrictions, protection of authentication information, transmission protection, hashing of participation tokens, retention-period management, and security reviews, to prevent loss, theft, leakage, forgery, alteration, or damage.
