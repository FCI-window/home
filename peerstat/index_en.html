PeerStat:A New Way Toward Fairer Evaluation

Better organizations need fairer evaluation.

Click a title to explore the PeerStat story.

01

Has our organization’s evaluation been fair?

+

Results and goal achievement can be measured with numbers, but isn’t it difficult to evaluate work attitudes and collaboration fairly as well?

Performance matters to every organization. But to create that performance, shouldn’t people receive fair evaluations they can accept?

An evaluation where the colleagues we want to work with are recognized—and where we can think, “I want to become that kind of colleague too.”

02

What kind of people do companies and colleagues want to work with?

+

Perhaps the person who first comes to mind when you hear these questions is someone you want to work with.

Helpfulness

Who is most helpful to colleagues and the organization?

Initiative

When something new comes up, who participates most actively?

Efficiency

Who stays most focused on work and uses working hours most effectively?

Wouldn’t you be curious to know how other people see you?

03

Why is it difficult to evaluate people’s actual work fairly?

+

Limited visibility

Colleagues who work together see each other’s work up close—but can evaluators see those details as well?

Limited anonymity

If people worry that their individual evaluations may be revealed, can they participate honestly?

Limited clarity in process and results

If evaluation is complicated or the final result appears only as individual rankings, could it lead to competition and conflict instead of constructive feedback?

04

PeerStat: Everyone evaluates, and only group results remain.

+
  1. 1
    An administrator creates an evaluation group, and members join together.
  2. 2
    For three core questions, members use touch and drag to rank everyone, including themselves.
  3. 3
    When evaluation is complete, individual evaluations are not revealed. Only group averages and distributions are provided.

Individual evaluations from colleagues are gathered and provided only as group results.

05

PeerStat complements existing evaluations.

+

Performance and goal achievement still matter.

PeerStat does not replace existing evaluations. It broadens the evidence by adding the perspectives of colleagues who work together.

When people believe they are being evaluated fairly, they can reflect on their work and attitude—and turn that reflection into positive changes in their next actions.

Performance matters to an organization.
Fair evaluation matters to the people who create that performance.

Everyone evaluates, and only group averages and distributions remain.

We gather the perspectives of people who work together to broaden the evidence for evaluation.
