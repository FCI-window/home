01

It began with a desire to live a “better” life.

+

It wasn't about having more money, a bigger house, or a faster car.

It began with a simple question: How could I live a life of greater meaning, a stronger sense of purpose, and greater fulfillment?

02

I worked in the field of wireless communication semiconductors—the technology that connects people.

+

I was involved in every stage of the process for the transceiver semiconductors used in mobile phones—from development through sales and marketing.

As mobile phones evolved from devices that only made calls into smartphones at the center of daily life, I felt it was a shame that such a powerful tool was used mainly as an output tool—watching videos, reading the news, or playing games.

I began exploring how to use the smartphone as an input tool—one that allows anyone to contribute something of their own and give it meaning.

I also studied React Native Expo and Firebase .

03
