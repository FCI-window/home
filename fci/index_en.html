01

It began with a desire to live a “better” life.

+

Not money, a big house, or a fast car.

It began with the question of how to live a more meaningful, rewarding, and fulfilling life.

02

We worked in the field of wireless communication semiconductors connecting people.

+

We experienced everything from designing transmit-and-receive semiconductors for mobile phones to sales and marketing.

As phones that could only make calls became smartphones and moved to the center of everyday life,

we felt it was a shame that such a powerful tool was mainly used for viewing, not for input.

So we began considering how to use the smartphone as an input tool.

03

We create tools through repeated questions of “Why?” and “How?”.

+

The company name, FCI, is short for Factory of Creative Imagination.
In Korean, it can be understood as a “creative imagination workshop.”
Our logo is shaped after the Chinese character ‘문(問)’, meaning “question.”
We keep asking “Why?” and “How?” as we search for answers.
For solutions the world has not seen before...

04
