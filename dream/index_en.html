01

What are “good habits”?

+

They say a fulfilling life begins with good habits. A gratitude journal to capture the day, reading to broaden our thoughts, exercise to awaken the body, and small routines in everyday life—could these be the habits we need?

02

Why can’t we keep good habits going?

+

Even when we want to build good habits, it is not easy. Missing a day or two can make it difficult to start again. Keeping a record can feel complicated, and motivation can quickly fade when we are doing it alone.

03

How can we make it work?

+

What if we recorded journaling, reading, exercise, and small daily routines together, in a very simple way? Doing it alone can be good, but what if we created groups where we could encourage one another and practice together? Above all, shouldn’t we be able to start again without pressure, even after missing a day or two?

04

DRE&M is here to help.

+

DRE&M is an app that brings your daily habits together in one place and helps you keep going. Record journaling, reading, exercise, and small daily routines simply and without pressure, so you can look back on today and prepare for tomorrow. You can also create groups, practice together, and motivate one another through friendly competition. Missing a few days is okay. Because your records are measured over the past 7 and 30 days, you can start again at any time. Looking back at your past records can also help you set bigger plans for the future.

05

Today’s small records shape tomorrow’s me.

+

DRE&M is not only for special people. Anyone can practice small habits at their own pace and record them simply. A single page of reading or one small action a day is enough. As these records add up, today’s daily life can gradually change, and they may help create a better tomorrow.

06
